### LinkedIn

> Shipped wasitus v0.1 — a small Go CLI for a question every on-call knows:
> **was it us, or outside our control?**
>
> It builds an incident timeline from OpenTelemetry/SigNoz and GitHub, then
> returns a deterministic verdict (`OUR_SIDE` / `EXTERNAL` / `UNKNOWN`) with
> evidence you can check. Rules decide; no LLM in the critical path.
>
> Highlights:
> - 8 staged scenarios in CI, including the red herring (harmless deploy +
>   real provider account failure → must not blame the deploy)
> - Read-only, runs on your machine, PII redaction on by default
> - Demo lab anyone can reproduce in ~10 minutes
>
> Install: `brew install alloteyphil/wasitus/wasitus`
> Repo: https://github.com/alloteyphil/wasitus
> Write-up: https://github.com/alloteyphil/wasitus/blob/main/docs/writeups/was-it-us.md
